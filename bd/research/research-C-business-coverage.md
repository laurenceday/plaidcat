## 1. Does Plaid officially support business accounts? YES — for *SMB online banking*, not treasury portals

- Plaid launched **"Transactions for Business"** (announced ~May 29, 2025; GA'd through 2025-2026): business account indicator + 13 business-specific transaction categories (Revenue, Payroll, Loan Payments, ...) in the Transactions API. Plaid's claim: **"95% of U.S. banks that serve small businesses"** and business accounts connectable at **4,500+ financial institutions**. — **OFFICIAL**: https://plaid.com/blog/transactions-for-business/ ; https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2025/05/29/plaid-launches-transactions-for-business-powering-real-time-business-tools/161839/ ; https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/plaid-rolls-out-transactions-for-business ; https://fintechmagazine.com/articles/plaid-expands-business-banking-data-coverage-in-us-market
- Plaid markets business-account coverage for lending: "personal and business account coverage… across the lending lifecycle," 12,000+ institutions total (US/CA/EU/UK). — **OFFICIAL**: https://plaid.com/resources/lending/embedded-lending-platforms-marketplaces/ ; https://plaid.com/use-cases/business-financial-management/
- Critical framing: Plaid's business support = the **same retail/SMB online-banking front door** (chase.com, bankofamerica.com small business, wellsfargo.com/biz) reached via the same OAuth APIs as consumer accounts. There is **no Plaid connectivity to commercial treasury platforms** (see §3). Third-party trackers state plainly: "Business accounts may have limited support" at Chase via Plaid. — **OPERATOR-REPORTED**: https://www.openbankingtracker.com/plaid/chase
- The **`account.holder_category`-style business/personal indicator** exists ("account indicator… whether the bank account linked is a business or personal account"). — **OFFICIAL**: https://plaid.com/blog/transactions-for-business/

### Treasury-management portals: the hard wall
JPMorgan Access / Chase Connect, BofA CashPro, Wells Fargo Vantage (ex-CEO), CitiDirect, U.S. Bank SinglePoint, PNC PINACLE are **separate authentication realms with no Plaid institution entries**. The sanctioned data paths are each bank's own **treasury APIs** (CashPro Information Reporting API, JPM Access/Developer Portal, CitiConnect), ERP connectors, or BAI2/ISO 20022 file feeds — all requiring the *account holder's* bank relationship, paperwork, and often fees. A mid-market corporate whose operating accounts live only in these portals is **invisible to Plaid**. — **OPERATOR-REPORTED + INFERRED** (sources in table).

## 3. Institution connectivity ≠ product support (mechanics for checking)

- `/institutions/get` and `/institutions/get_by_id` return a **`products` array per institution** — an institution may list `transactions, auth, balance` but not `statements` or `identity`. Calling an unsupported product returns **`PRODUCTS_NOT_SUPPORTED`**. — **OFFICIAL**: https://plaid.com/docs/api/institutions/
- **`institution_status` / health breakdown**: per-product status objects (Auth, Balance, Identity, Investments, Liabilities, Transactions updates, Item logins) each HEALTHY / DEGRADED / DOWN, with refresh-frequency data; also surfaced in the Dashboard and in Link itself. — **OFFICIAL**: https://plaid.com/docs/link/institution-status/ ; https://support.plaid.com/hc/en-us/articles/15141515967639 ; https://plaid.com/blog/institution-item-system-status/
- **Statements** (bank-branded PDF retrieval, US only) is the narrowest product: supported at **Bank of America, Chase (early availability — gated, request via account manager), Citibank, Fifth Third, Huntington, Navy FCU, Regions, Truist, US Bank, Wells Fargo** plus some smaller banks/CUs. Plaid's own docs say Statements "does not currently support all major or long-tail institutions and should be used with a fallback option." — **OFFICIAL**: https://plaid.com/docs/statements/
- **Identity** on business accounts: returns account-holder name with match scoring; the docs expose an `is_business_name`-style flag keyed off "LLC/INC/CORP/LTD" strings — i.e., business-name matching is heuristic, and entity-name vs signer-name mismatches are an expected failure class for KYB-style checks. — **OFFICIAL**: https://plaid.com/docs/api/products/identity/
- Practical consequence: build the coverage check as **(institution supports product) AND (product currently HEALTHY) AND (account is business per holder indicator)** — not just "bank is in Plaid."

## 4. Multi-user / multi-entity / MFA behavior

- **Sub-user & view-only logins are the #1 silent killer.** Plaid help center (BofA article): only the **primary account holder's username** can link business accounts; delegated users get "no eligible accounts." Treasury teams almost never hand out the owner credential. — **OFFICIAL**: https://support.plaid.com/hc/en-us/articles/25349199961879
- **OAuth account selection**: Chase/BofA/WF force per-account consent screens; users can (and do) share a subset, and later additions require **update mode with account selection re-run**. "No eligible accounts" also fires when `account_filters` excludes what the business shared. — **OFFICIAL**: https://plaid.com/docs/link/oauth/ ; https://support-my.plaid.com/hc/en-us/articles/9098878272663-No-eligible-accounts
- **One login, many LLCs**: works only if all entities' accounts are visible under a single online-banking profile at that bank (common at Chase Business, Mercury, Relay); each separately-credentialed entity = separate Link item = separate borrower friction. **INFERRED** from account-selection mechanics.
- **Hardware tokens / dual approval**: under OAuth the bank's own login page handles any MFA (incl. Wells Fargo's RSA SecurID for business online), so token MFA is survivable *where OAuth exists*; on legacy credential connections, device-bound OTP/token MFA historically caused loops and failures. Dual-approval/maker-checker portals (SinglePoint Company-ID+2FA, CitiBusiness token) sit outside OAuth and are exactly the ones that fail. — **INFERRED + OPERATOR-REPORTED**: https://www.wellsfargo.com/biz/online-banking/securid/ ; https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/banking/us-bank-singlepoint/00/1057970
- **Consent expiry = recurring re-auth debt**: BofA Items expire after **12 months**; PNC Auth Items after **1 year**; renewals require sending the borrower through Link update mode. UK: 90-day bank re-auth replaced by TPP-side consent reconfirmation (FCA change), which Plaid handles but the app must trigger. — **OFFICIAL**: https://plaid.com/docs/link/oauth/ ; https://plaid.com/blog/90-day-are-you-ready/

## 5. Known failure modes for business accounts (triangulated)

1. **Wrong front door**: company banks at JPM/BofA/WF/Citi *commercial* → institution literally not in Plaid search (CashPro not findable in QB; Vantage hidden in Plaid & MX). — **OPERATOR-REPORTED** (§2 sources)
2. **Entitlement lockout**: bookkeeper/CFO sub-login connects fine to the bank but yields zero linkable accounts (BofA policy; similar patterns reported at other banks). — **OFFICIAL**
3. **Separate-institution confusion**: user picks "Citibank" instead of "CitiBusiness Online" (or vice versa) → login fails or wrong account set; Cross River splits personal/business the same way. — **OPERATOR-REPORTED**
4. **ITEM_LOGIN_REQUIRED churn**: password resets, T&C interstitials, bank-side security blocks — items die and need update-mode; on business accounts with shared credentials this is frequent. — **OFFICIAL**: https://plaid.com/docs/errors/item/
5. **Screen-scraping sunset**: majors have all moved to OAuth/API (Plaid signed API-migration agreements 2023-2025; JPMC now **charges Plaid for data** under 2025 agreements, with Wells/PNC/Fidelity/Schwab following) — long-tail business portals that never get APIs simply rot. — **OFFICIAL/PRESS**: https://www.pymnts.com/news/digital-banking/2023/plaid-signs-agreements-migrate-traffic-financial-institutions-apis/ ; https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/plaid-to-pay-for-jpmorgan-data-open-banking-fintechs/760192/ ; https://riabiz.com/a/2025/11/8/jp-morgan-hustles-plaid-for-a-deal-in-bitter-fight-over-free-data-with-wells-and-pnc-encouraged-while-fidelity-schwab-also-force-fintechs-to-pay-up-or-pound-sand-as-part-of-deeper-battle
6. **What SMB lenders actually do**: Ramp, Mercury, Bluevine, Relay, Meow all use Plaid for bank linking (Ramp also ships Plaid Pay-by-Bank); embedded-lending literature (Parafin, Pipe, Wayflyer ecosystem) describes **Plaid/Finicity bank data + accounting (QBO/Xero) + platform revenue data**, with manual document upload as the universal fallback. — **OFFICIAL/OPERATOR-REPORTED**: https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/46335537186707-Enabling-Plaid-s-Pay-by-Bank-for-Ramp-Business-Account ; https://www.apideck.com/blog/embedded-lending-revenue-financing-banking-accounting-commerce-data ; https://www.lendflow.com/post/smb-underwriting-data

## 7. Product-level coverage differences for business accounts

- **Balance / Auth**: broadest — works wherever the institution connects at all (it's the core of every integration). — OFFICIAL (institutions pages list Auth/Balance ubiquitously)
- **Transactions**: broad; now enriched for business (indicator + business categories, beta→GA 2025-26). Refresh cadence varies by institution (visible in health breakdown). — OFFICIAL
- **Assets** (point-in-time report incl. balances+transactions+owner): effectively wherever Transactions works; the standard SMB-underwriting artifact. — OFFICIAL: https://plaid.com/docs/api/products/ (Assets)
- **Identity**: supported at majors but **person-oriented**; business-name detection is heuristic (`LLC/INC` string match); expect entity-vs-signer mismatches. — OFFICIAL: https://plaid.com/docs/api/products/identity/
- **Statements**: narrowest — ~10 named large banks (Chase still gated "early availability") + assorted small FIs; Plaid itself says to build a fallback. Fintech business banks (Mercury/Relay/etc.) generally **not** Statements-supported. — OFFICIAL: https://plaid.com/docs/statements/
- Rule of thumb: **Balance ⊇ Transactions ⊇ Assets ≫ Identity > Statements** for business accounts.

## 10. Three biggest coverage risks & practical mitigations

**Risk 1 — The treasury-portal wall: the bigger the borrower, the less Plaid sees.**
Mid-market and larger corporates bank through JPM Access/Chase Connect, CashPro, Vantage, CitiDirect, SinglePoint, PINACLE — none reachable by Plaid, Finicity, MX, or Akoya. Exactly the borrowers with the largest credit lines have the least aggregator-visible cash.
*Mitigations*: (a) make **borrower-provisioned direct bank API / ERP feed** (CashPro API, JPM Access API, BAI2/ISO 20022 SFTP) a first-class integration path in covenant tooling; (b) contractually require borrowers to hold a **monitorable operating account** (e.g., a Chase Business/Mercury account in Plaid scope) that reference flows transit; (c) accept **auditor-style monthly statement upload (PDF + parsing)** as the covenant-grade fallback with tamper checks.

**Risk 2 — Entitlement & consent decay: links that never form or silently die.**
Primary-owner-only linking (BofA policy, similar elsewhere), OAuth partial account selection, 12-month consent expiry (BofA, PNC), password/MFA resets → ITEM_LOGIN_REQUIRED. For a credit protocol, a dead Item during a drawdown is a covenant-monitoring outage, not an inconvenience.
*Mitigations*: (a) onboarding runbook that requires the **credential owner** (not the bookkeeper) to perform Link and to share **all** operating accounts (verify count vs. declared accounts); (b) monitor `consent_expiration_time` + Item webhooks and drive proactive update-mode re-auth 30 days early, with loan-agreement obligations to re-link within N days; (c) reconcile Plaid-visible balances against borrower-attested totals to detect hidden accounts.

**Risk 3 — Single-aggregator and product-gap dependence.**
Plaid's per-institution product gaps (Statements at ~10 big banks only; Identity business-name heuristics), per-institution health volatility (CitiBusiness-class breakage), and the new **pay-for-data regime** (JPMC charging aggregators; Wells/PNC/Fidelity/Schwab following) create both coverage holes and pricing/continuity risk.
*Mitigations*: (a) **multi-aggregator abstraction** — route long-tail/business portals to Finicity (covers CitiBusiness Online) or MX when Plaid's `/institutions/get` product array or health status fails a pre-flight check; (b) gate underwriting features per-institution using `products` + `institution_status` at Link time rather than assuming uniform capability; (c) keep the **manual statement-upload + parsing pipeline** (and for crypto-native borrowers, on-chain attestations/proof-of-reserves style checks) as the universal floor so no borrower is unsupportable.
