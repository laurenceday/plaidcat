Part II · Data products

The data: what Wildcat can actually obtain

Transactions, Assets and Statements do different jobs, bill differently, and share differently. Which of them carries a persistent borrower cash-flow dataset, and what the fine print does to the two-year-history ambition.

1 · Transactions, the workhorse

The one product built for a persistent, continuously updated dataset. Everything below is verified against the OpenAPI spec unless labeled otherwise. official

  • Lookback. days_requested runs 1 to 730, default 90, set once at Link and immutable afterward. Getting this wrong means deleting the Item and asking the borrower to re-link. The 730 is a request ceiling, not a promise: Capital One serves 90 days per the docs official, and operators report Bank of America around 18 months on checking operator-reported. Real two-year depth accrues by holding the connection and letting the dataset age in.
  • Fields. Amounts (positive means outflow, a sign-convention trap), posted and authorized dates, merchant name, counterparties with stable entity ids and confidence levels, the personal-finance category taxonomy, location, payment channel, pending lifecycle, and the raw bank descriptor when include_original_description is switched on. Switch it on: B2B wires and ACH descriptors mostly resolve as low-confidence cleansed strings, and the raw string is what a classifier needs.
  • Sync. /transactions/sync returns added, modified and removed arrays with a cursor. Plaid polls institutions one to four times a day; SYNC_UPDATES_AVAILABLE announces every change; /transactions/refresh forces a pull for a fee.
  • Recurring. /transactions/recurring detects inflow and outflow streams with predicted next dates and frequencies; ask for at least 180 days of history for it to work well.
  • Business betas. "Transactions for Business" (May 2025) added a business-account indicator (about 70% populated) and 13 business categories: Revenue, Payroll, Loan Payments and the rest. Both are gated through an account manager. The spec also hides a business_finance_category on every transaction. operator-reported
Found in the spec

An entire undocumented /cashflow_report/* endpoint family (get, refresh, transactions, insights, 730-day range) sits hidden in Plaid's current OpenAPI build. It reads as an unreleased packaged cash-flow report, plausibly the business-side counterpart to the consumer Check product. If it ships, it could collapse some of the assemble-it-yourself work this site describes. Existence verified in the spec; a full mid-2026 mirror of plaid.com/docs contains zero references, so it remains undocumented, and the closest documented analog is Check's consumer-side Cash Flow Updates monitoring beta. Everything beyond existence is unverified.

2 · Assets, the artifact

  • An Asset Report is an immutable point-in-time snapshot over 1 to 99 Items and up to 731 days: balances, daily historical balances, the full transaction window, and account-owner identity. Generation is asynchronous, announced by webhook, and the report renders to a bank-formatted PDF. official
  • Assets is best initialized at Link, and the docs also allow adding it to an existing Item through a short update-mode consent step (users connected under two years ago skip the credentials pane). Refreshing means buying a new report. History past 61 days adds a flat per-Item fee, each block of five Items adds a surcharge, and the PDF and Audit Copy each carry their own per-request fee. Reports have no documented expiry; they persist until removed.
  • Sharing is the point. Audit Copy hands a fixed auditor (Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, Ocrolus) direct access. Relay shares the exact report, JSON and PDF and even refresh rights, with any third party that holds its own Plaid client id. These are the sanctioned rails for handing verified reports to downstream parties, and the recipient must be Plaid-registered. official
  • Fit: origination and covenant checkpoints. As a monitoring feed it is the wrong shape and the wrong bill.

3 · Statements, the fallback

The product is the PDFs: exact bank-branded statements with SHA-256 hashes, listed and downloaded by API, up to two years requested in one Link session and extendable forward by refresh. Like Assets, it can be added to an existing Item through an update-mode consent step, and the docs recommend requesting it as required-if-supported so unsupported institutions do not block Link. official

The brief asked directly

Could Wildcat pull about two years of corporate operating-account statements automatically after one connect? Only partially. Mechanically yes at a supported institution: one Link session with a 24-month window returns the PDFs, and monthly refreshes extend forward. The limits: coverage is roughly ten large banks plus assorted small ones, around 40% of US depository accounts, with Chase still gated behind early availability; depository accounts only; Plaid's own docs instruct integrators to build a fallback; per-institution statement depth varies; and whether business operating accounts work reliably per institution is unverified. Fintech business banks (Mercury, Relay and kin) generally lack Statements support entirely.

4 · The rest of the catalog

Balance

Cached balances free on /accounts/get; real-time pulls on /accounts/balance/get billed per call. Spot checks before disbursement, nothing more. Balance Plus appears folded into Signal; its risk schemas left the spec. unverified

Identity

Account-holder names, emails, phones, addresses; only names are guaranteed. Business-account detection now runs through the beta holder_category field (about 70% populated) plus an undeprecated business-name flag on /identity/match; whether the name returned is the entity or the signer still depends on the institution. Plan for reconciliation, not truth. official

Signal

Per-payment ACH return risk with 80-plus attributes. Repayment-collection tooling, not monitoring. Requires its own consent since July 2026.

Income / Bank Income

Models a person's income, not a company's revenue. Plaid now steers new US integrations toward Consumer Report. Not a foundation here.

Plaid Check / Consumer Report

Plaid's own FCRA consumer reporting agency: cash-flow attributes, LendScore, 24-hour report TTL, monitoring subscriptions. Consumer only. For business-entity credit it is inapplicable; using it on guarantors drags in FCRA duties. See the legal page.

Enrich & processor tokens

/transactions/enrich runs Plaid's categorization over transactions obtained elsewhere, useful for statement-parsed or direct-feed data. Processor tokens expose nearly the full product surface to a Plaid partner per Item, but there is no processor Assets; report sharing goes through Relay.

6 · Foundation verdict

What carries the dataset

Transactions is the foundation; Assets is the underwriting artifact; Statements is documentary evidence where it exists. Initialize every Item with days_requested: 730, raw descriptors on, and the business betas requested. Generate Asset Reports at origination and covenant checkpoints for an immutable, shareable, PDF-able record. Pull Statements where supported as bank-authenticated proof. Check, Income and Signal are explicitly not foundations for a business-entity data layer. Institution history caps mean the two-year dataset is partly harvested and partly grown: connect early, keep the Item alive, and month 25 onward becomes history nobody else can re-pull.

A docs-level usage restriction to carry into the Plaid negotiation

Plaid's Financial Insights docs state that Transactions, Enrich, Liabilities and Investments "may not be used as part of a credit or underwriting decisioning process", and the migration guides steer US consumer underwriting to the FCRA-side Consumer Report. Business-entity credit sits outside that consumer product's scope, and Plaid's own underwriting page recommends Assets exactly for "use cases not supported by Consumer Report". The practical consequence: Wildcat's written use-case approval should name Assets as the underwriting artifact and Transactions as the monitoring feed, in those words, so the decisioning-restriction language never becomes an argument later. official
