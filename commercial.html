Commercials

What it costs and what it takes to get in

Plaid will not be a material line item. The binding constraints are procedural (production approval of a DeFi-adjacent use case) and contractual (auto-renewal, rate rights, no SLA), not price. Numbers below mix official structure with operator-reported price points; labels mark which is which.

1 · How Plaid bills

  • Plans. Free Trial (ten live production Items), pay-as-you-go with no minimum, Growth with a minimum and annual commit, Custom above that. Rates appear at production signup, not in public. official
  • Three billing models. One-time per Item (Auth about $1.00 to $1.25, Identity about $1.10), subscription per Item per month (Transactions about $0.25 to $0.30 reported), and per-request or flexible (Balance about $0.05 to $0.15 per call, Asset Reports about $3 to $5, Statements scaled by statement count). operator-reported
  • The 2-year backfill is cheap. History past 61 days costs one flat Additional History fee per Item, whatever the depth. official
  • The billing gotchas. An Item in an error state keeps billing its subscription until removed; Plaid's own docs warn about being billed indefinitely. No pro-rating. Asset reports covering more than five Items multiply the fee. Re-links can re-trigger one-time fees. official
  • Rate limits are irrelevant at Wildcat scale, except the five-per-minute per-Item balance check, which only bites naive polling. official

2 · The Wildcat cost model

Assumptions: two Items per borrower; onboarding pulls identity and a 730-day history; monitoring runs the transactions subscription with two on-demand refreshes per Item per month; one asset report per borrower per quarter; statements monthly where supported (a model assumption of about 60% of institutions; the products page carries the ~40%-of-depository-accounts coverage figure). 3 · Getting approved, including the crypto question

  • Process. Dashboard application, use-case review by Plaid's Customer Oversight team, billing setup, about a week, then per-institution OAuth registration; the security questionnaire gates certain banks official and is effectively SOC 2-shaped inferred.
  • Crypto is not a policy bar. Plaid names Coinbase and Gemini as customers, runs a dedicated crypto use-case page, and added read-only exchange data in 2022 official. No prohibited-industries bar was found on the data products, a searched absence rather than a verified negative inferred; the Transfer payments product does carry one official. No rejection reports of crypto firms surfaced.
  • The residual risk is contractual. The MSA permits immediate suspension for legal violations or reputational harm, and the Developer Policy reserves broad termination rights. A DeFi credit protocol is a novel oversight profile: expect deeper diligence and slower onboarding, and present the use case as commercial-lending underwriting and monitoring with a clean borrower consent flow. Get the use case, including derived-metric publication, approved in writing in the order form. inferred

4 · Contract terms that matter

Renewal mechanics

Twelve-month initial term, auto-renewing yearly; non-renewal needs 60 days' notice; Plaid may raise any rate at renewal on 30 days' notice; operators report 5-10% annual escalators operator-reported. Calendar the notice window. official on the MSA terms.

No SLA

The standard MSA carries no uptime commitment and none is published anywhere. For a protocol whose covenants depend on monitoring feeds, this is the real exposure; negotiate service credits or design for staleness. inferred from absence

Money terms

Net-15, 1.5% monthly late interest, payments non-refundable and non-cancellable; an unused minimum is sunk. Termination for material breach carries only a 10-day cure. official

Exit mechanics

On termination: 30 days to download client data, deletion on request, and already-pulled end-user data stays in Wildcat's systems but remains bound by the MSA's use limits. Convert raw pulls into derived underwriting records before any exit; Items never port between aggregators, though a small corporate borrower base re-links by email campaign. official

On the bank-fee overhang: JPMorgan's per-pull fees are live and Plaid signed, saying publicly that customer contracts and pricing are unchanged at this time. No premium-institution surcharge has been found as of August 2026; absence of evidence, not proof. The pass-through vehicle, if it comes, is the renewal-rate clause. inferred

5 · Three commercial risks

Risk 1 · Approval and unilateral termination

Plaid happily serves crypto exchanges, but a DeFi credit protocol is novel to its oversight team, and the MSA's suspension rights hang over the integration permanently. Mitigate with the written use-case approval, derived records outside the raw-data restrictions, and a warm second aggregator path.

Risk 2 · Renewal-time escalation

The industry now has a legitimized bank-fee model and Plaid holds a unilateral renewal-rate right. Budget the escalator, calendar the 60-day window, and keep usage portable.

Risk 3 · No SLA plus silent meters

No uptime commitment, errored Items billing forever, multiplier fees on big reports. An ops-discipline tax rather than a dollar risk at this scale, but the SLA gap matters if covenants depend on the feed.
